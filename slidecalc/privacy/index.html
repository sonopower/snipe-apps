Axle Weight & Slide Calc — Privacy Policy

Work out the tandem slide from a scale ticket, before you pull off the scale.

The short version

Axle Weight & Slide Calc collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and it makes no network requests at all. Nothing you type leaves your device.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is written to your device's own storage and to your device backup if you have backups turned on. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, because the app has no way to send it.

  • Weighings you save — the axle weights you typed and the result the app worked out.
  • Rigs you set up — hole spacing, axle spacing and the names you give them.
  • Your settings: units, appearance, and whether the app is unlocked.

What we receive

Nothing. We have no server. We cannot see your data, and we could not hand it over to anyone if we were asked to, because we do not have it.

Apple processes your purchase and may share aggregate sales and crash statistics with us as the developer. That happens under Apple's privacy policy, not ours, and it never identifies you.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It contains no advertising and no links out to social networks.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. There is no copy anywhere else to request the deletion of.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, a change would mean the app started doing something new — and that would ship as a visible app update.
