Axle Weight & Slide Calc — Support

Work out the tandem slide from a scale ticket, before you pull off the scale.

How to

Read the answer

Type the steer, drive and trailer weights off the scale ticket. The headline tells you which way to slide and how many holes; the diagram shows the move; the badge tells you whether the rig is legal after it.

Which way is FORWARD

Forward means toward the tractor. Sliding the trailer tandems forward moves weight off the drives and onto the trailer axles; sliding them back does the opposite. Slide toward the group that is overweight to lighten it.

Where the limits come from

The Limits screen names its source and the date it was checked: 80,000 lb gross, 20,000 lb single, 34,000 lb tandem and the federal bridge formula from 23 CFR 658.17, plus California's kingpin rule. These are the federal Interstate limits. Non-Interstate state routes can be lower — always verify against current state law.

Set up your own trailer

Rigs let you save hole spacing and axle spacing per trailer, so the hole count matches the trailer you are actually pulling.

Get the numbers out

Weigh history exports to CSV from the history screen for your own records.
