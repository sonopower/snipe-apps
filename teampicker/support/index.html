Team Generator: Random Picker — Support

Fair teams and random name draws for classrooms, coaches and trainers.

How to

Make fair teams

Open the Teams tab, choose how many teams you want, and tap Make teams. Add a skill rating to any name on the Rosters tab and the split balances on that rating instead of splitting at random.

Draw names without repeats

The Wheel tab draws from the roster without replacement — nobody comes up twice until the pool empties. Tap Undo to put the last name back.

Paste a class list

Rosters tab, Add roster, then paste names one per line. Anything you can copy out of a spreadsheet or a gradebook works.

Show it on the projector

Tap Projector mode for oversized, high-contrast cards that read from the back of a room.

Move to a new device

Export a roster to CSV from the Rosters tab and import it on the other device. Rosters are stored only on the device, so they do not travel on their own.

Restore your purchase

Settings, then Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account that bought the unlock; the unlock is a one-time purchase and never expires.

Still stuck?

Email ovy8power@gmail.com and say which device and iOS version you are on. Replies usually go out within two business days.

The app works completely offline, so there is nothing to check on our end and no service that can be down.
